Ask ChatGPT about Trola, by voice too: "How many kilometres did I drive yesterday?", "How much energy did Trola use on Sunday?", "Where did I charge yesterday?", "Where did we sleep last night?", "What's the battery at?" ChatGPT gets the answers from TrolaSpy through a private link. It reads totals, charging, the battery, and where Trola is, charged, stopped and slept (as place names), but not the route or photos.

Add it to ChatGPT

  1. On a computer, open chatgpt.com. In Settings, open Apps, then Advanced settings, and turn on Developer mode.
  2. Click Create app. Name it TrolaSpy, paste the link above as the MCP server URL, and choose No authentication.
  3. Accept the warning and create it. ChatGPT shows TrolaSpy's tools, which only read.
  4. In a chat, pick TrolaSpy from the + menu, or start voice and ask about Trola.
Anyone with the link can ask these questions, so keep it to yourself. New link replaces it (paste the new one into the app's settings in ChatGPT); Turn off stops it.
The solar charger and the DC-DC charger send their readings over Bluetooth. The TrolaSpy Bridge app on the tablet passes them on. Download the app. In the app, enter the server … and LeafSpy's ID and password (LeafSpy: Settings, Server).
Each charger encrypts its readings. To get its key, open it in VictronConnect, tap the gear icon, then Product info. Turn on Instant readout via Bluetooth, then tap Show next to Instant readout details and copy the encryption key.

What the tablet hears

One point per day. SOH and Hx are LeafSpy's battery health figures; capacity is the pack's usable amp-hours (AHr).
